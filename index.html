Our Story

Weaving Punjabi Tradition into Tomorrow

Pehnawa Panjab began with a simple vision: to preserve the intricate artistry of Punjabi heritage while reimagining it for the modern world. We partner with skilled artisans across Punjab, honouring their craft and bringing you authentic handcrafted Punjabi suits, Phulkari dupattas, juttis and kundan jewellery designed to be cherished for a lifetime.
